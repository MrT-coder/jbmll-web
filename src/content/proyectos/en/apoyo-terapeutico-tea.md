I built a therapeutic support tool that detects and helps prevent social crises
in people with autism spectrum disorder, using artificial intelligence and
real-time computer vision.

## Emotion recognition

I implemented non-intrusive facial emotion recognition with a quantized
MobileNetV2 model on TensorFlow Lite, OpenCV and MediaPipe. Non-intrusive
matters: any device the person has to wear changes the very behavior you want to
observe.

On top of that I built interactive narrative scenarios, in a visual novel
format, adapted by age.

## Post-session feedback

I integrated a generative model that produces therapeutic feedback after each
session, with speech synthesis to deliver it.

## Validation

It was validated at the Centro Caminito with professionals in the field. The tool
showed high technical stability on standard hardware, with a mean time between
failures of 57.6 minutes, and usability rated as good: 70.5 on the SUS scale.

The work was published as a peer-reviewed chapter in Lecture Notes in Computer
Science.
